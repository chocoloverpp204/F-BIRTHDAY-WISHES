A LETTER FROM THE HEART

Dearest Koko Ahmed,

Happy Birthday! ♡

I hope today brings a genuine smile to your face and reminds you that you deserve happiness, kindness, and all the good things life has to offer.

I wanted to make something a little different for your birthday — a small world filled with wishes, surprises, and words chosen especially for you. Something that could make you smile and remind you how special you are to me.

Honestly, I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me. You are not just someone special in my life; you are someone who has found a place in my heart that nobody else could ever replace. You have become one of the most important people in my world, someone whose happiness matters to me more than I can explain.

To me, you are the best man I could ever wish for. Not because I believe you have to be perfect, but because there is something about you that makes my heart choose you, again and again. I appreciate you for who you are, for the person you have shown me, and for the feelings you have brought into my life. If someone asked me what makes you so special, I honestly wouldn't know where to begin, because there are so many little things about you that my heart has grown to love.

I love you more deeply than I sometimes know how to express. I love you in ways that go beyond beautiful words and sweet moments. I care about your happiness, your peace, your dreams, and the future you hope to build. I want to see you succeed, achieve the things you've always wished for, and become the person you dream of being. Even when I cannot do much, I will always wish the best for you and pray for your happiness.

You have become such an important part of my life that even the smallest things can remind me of you. Sometimes, I wish I could explain everything my heart feels, but the words never seem enough. All I know is that what I feel for you is sincere, and you mean far more to me than you might ever realise.

If there is one thing I want you to know, it is that you are precious to me. I don't love you because I expect you to be flawless, and I don't need you to be someone other than yourself. I simply cherish you for who you are. You have a special place in my heart, and no matter how difficult it may sometimes be to express my feelings, that place will always mean something beautiful to me.

May Allah bless you with good health, peace, happiness, and success in everything you do. May He guide you towards what is best for you, make your difficult days easier, protect your heart from unnecessary pain, and fill your future with countless blessings. May He grant you the things that are truly good for you, fulfil your sincere duas, and lead you towards a future filled with goodness. Ameen.

I hope this new chapter brings you closer to your dreams and gives you memories worth keeping. Never forget to take care of yourself, to be proud of how far you've come, and to appreciate the little moments that make life beautiful. You deserve a life filled with peace, genuine happiness, and people who appreciate the person you are.

And if I could wish for one more thing, it would be that you never have to question how much you matter. You matter to me more than these paragraphs could ever explain. Your happiness means something to me, your sadness matters to me, and your dreams are things I genuinely hope to see you achieve. I want life to be kind to you, even on the days when everything feels difficult.

Thank you for being someone so special in my world. Thank you for being the person my heart holds so dearly. I hope this little gift makes your birthday a little brighter and gives you a reason to smile. I may not be able to give you everything in the world, but I wanted to give you something made with my own time, effort, and heart — something that reminds you that you are loved and cherished.

Happy Birthday once again, dearest Koko Ahmed. ♡

May this year bring you countless beautiful moments, may Allah always protect and guide you, and may your heart find the happiness it deserves. No matter how many birthdays come and go, I hope you always remember that you are someone incredibly special to me.

You are my favourite person, someone I cherish deeply, and one of the most important people in my life. The person I hope to never lose, the only hand I wish to hold for the rest of my life. I love you more than these words could ever fully express. ♡

With all my love,
